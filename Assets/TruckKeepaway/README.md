# Truck Keepaway

2–4 free-for-all competitors. Choose30/60/90seconds cumulative possession, default60. First to the exact cumulative threshold wins.

Movement only. Whole-pad trigger difference brakes then reverses; shared stick uses its own Y axis. Chase the physical neutral ball or make a meaningful fresh body impact against its carrier.

A successful steal atomically transfers ownership, then explodes the old carrier. Sustained overlap, pooled debris and blast motion cannot recursively steal. The thief is protected from the immediate explosion. The old carrier respawns after about2seconds with a visible protection window and no free pickup. Escaped/overturned-carrier recovery drops a reachable neutral ball.

Pause and source disconnect preserve live physics, attempts and scores. Reconnect the same device or explicitly assign a replacement, release controls, then freshly confirm. Rematch retains setup. Shared audio/steering/vibration/display settings apply. No online services.

Build through LWS.TruckSports.Editor.SportsBuilder.Build with the explicit saved Sports scenes. Scene regeneration uses this sport’s owned Editor authoring utility and shared SportsEventAuthoring.

Vendor foundation: NWH/GR3D visuals and WheelController approach; existing Cheese blast/debris pools; Heat/TMP and Cinemachine. One physical ball and one carrier authority.

Validation and remaining human/device checks: see Assets/TruckSports/VALIDATION.md and Documentation/TruckSports/ExpansionCheckpoint.md. Development smoke runners execute only with their explicit validation flags and are inert in normal play. No physical-controller or human playtest acceptance is claimed.
