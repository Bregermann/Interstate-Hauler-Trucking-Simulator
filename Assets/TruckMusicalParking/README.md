# Truck Musical Parking

Eight actual NWH trucks circle an asphalt lot while original looping music plays. The public horn opens one fewer bay than the number of surviving competitors. Fit the whole truck footprint inside, upright and below 0.9m/s, for one continuous second. Before capture, physical pushing can displace a contender; after capture the truck remains a solid protected obstruction. A truck can secure only one space. Seven eliminations produce one winner.

Movement steers and accelerates; reverse input brakes before reversing. No action button. Up to four local humans use independent keyboard clusters, whole controllers or separate sticks, with AI filling eight competitors. All-AI works without devices. Names, colors and difficulty persist through rematches.

Music lasts an unpredictable 5–12 seconds. AI cannot inspect its scheduled stop and reacts only after the horn. During music, drive the marked outer loop. Eight metres of valid forward progress, recent movement and being outside a bay at the horn establish eligibility. Camping incurs eight metres of loop progress before capture, with visible warnings and no punitive teleport. Muted play has closed/open bay colors and strong phase text. Music has a separate shared volume setting.

Scrambles last at most 28 seconds. If several remain unparked, eliminate only the contender with the lowest *current valid* capture progress, then greatest distance to a free bay, then rotating seeded priority. Displaced progress is not banked. All other contenders advance. Exact simultaneous claims use earliest continuous observation and rotating seeded priority, independent of callback order.

Saved scene: `Assets/TruckMusicalParking/Scenes/TruckMusicalParking.unity`. Regenerate with `LWS.TruckMusicalParking.Editor.ParkingAuthoring.Create`. Uses installed GR3D trucks, NWH wheel physics and engine/horn audio, RoadPropsPack barriers, Toon Gas Station lamps, shared Heat/TMP setup and Cinemachine broadcast framing. Original Parking Polka is generated by the owned authoring utility. No vendor source changes or new packages.
