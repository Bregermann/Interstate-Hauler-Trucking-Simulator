# Truck Musical Parking

Eight actual NWH trucks circle the marked yellow band to original looping music. The public horn opens one fewer bay than surviving contenders. Fit the whole physical chassis footprint inside the painted boundary, upright, supported and below0.9m/s for one continuous second. Cosmetics never enlarge the footprint; paint has0.08m tolerance. A progress strip and participant card show capture. Before locking, physical contact may displace contenders. After capture the truck remains a protected solid obstacle with exactly one owned space.

At the28s timeout EVERY participant without their own secured bay is eliminated from one ownership snapshot. Partial progress does not count. The next round has survivors minus one bays. One survivor wins. If no bay was captured, replay the same roster with no elimination. There is no fixed seven-round requirement. Rematch preserves names, colors, sources and difficulties.

Movement steers/accelerates; reverse brakes before reversing. No action button is required. Up to four humans use independent keyboard clusters, separate controllers or opposite shared sticks; AI fills eight competitors. AI reacts to the same public horn, finishes moving the complete truck inside before braking, invalidates captured targets immediately and routes locally around protected trucks using the same physical controls.

Music lasts5–12 seconds. Inside cutting, early bay entry and sustained camping earn a3-second capture lock; repeated offenses escalate to6seconds. The offender must return to the yellow band before the timer can run, and it only runs DURING PARKING. It cannot be served during music. Boundary tolerance, offense debounce and brief actual incoming-truck impact recovery apply equally to humans/AI. Red padlock/countdown and eligibility text distinguish penalty, slow-down, incomplete fit and an already secured bay. No penalty teleport.

The camera fits active contenders and active bays, tightens smoothly during the scramble and excludes eliminated spectator parking positions. Muted play retains closed/open colors and public horn text. Pause/disconnect preserves motion, music position, capture and ownership; replacement requires neutral/fresh input.
