This update extends the saved Truck Sports collection, front end, settings, Billiards and Hockey. Hockey is now two teams of three. Use the saved scenes and LWS.TruckSports.Editor.SportsBuilder.Build for delivery. CreateValidateBuild reauthors front-end/Hockey content; it is not a safe validation-only command. ## Implementation and boundaries

Six hockey slots each retain a name, color, human/AI source and independent AI difficulty. Presets cover six keyboards, six whole controllers, three controllers with independently owned left/right sticks, AI-only and mixed custom teams. Active keyboard keys must be unique and cannot use Escape. A whole controller is exclusive; complementary sticks may belong to different participants. Both owners of a disconnected shared pad are identified and preserved. Reconnect/modal transitions require neutral then fresh input. Shared-pad reconnect confirmation observes current device state after attachment and accepts only a fresh confirmation edge; held South cannot resume or reassign either owner. Input Check displays all six sources; keyboard rollover and physical controller compatibility need hardware testing.

Hockey remains one PhysX collision model: steering/traction acceleration, local-velocity opposite-throttle braking, physical truck/puck contact, relative-impact feedback and upright bodies. No second collision impulse, shoot, pass, boost or dedicated brake input was added. Each team coordinates a challenger, support and defender with hysteresis. Team scores remain two entries; participants, readiness, spawns, identity, input and collision records use six entries. Recovery checks all other trucks, clears the recovered truck's old puck contacts and preserves the rest of the match. Physics queries have 64-entry buffers plus an allocating full-query fallback on saturation. Deadlock recovery requires prolonged immobility, actual pushing contacts and no accessible escape direction; ordinary stationary accessible pucks and temporary defensive blocks remain live.

Billiards keeps its own rules, formats, Practice, ghost trucks, feedback and hot-seat behavior. Both named participants may be human or AI with independent difficulty. AI navigation recovers and relaxes contact geometry after repeated failures, then attempts a real bumper strike rather than forfeiting. Hard strategy tuning is retained. Off-turn AI roams harmlessly and stops for modals. Normal human turn changes and settling preserve held drive and momentum; modal/reconnect/placement/hot-seat gates remain deliberate. Reconnected pads clear stale button history before accepting confirmation.

The actual opening bumper contact alone applies configurable 1.65 boost and 24 speed cap; normal shots retain 15 cap. CCD and swept pocket checks protect fast contacts. The eight-on-break respot policy remains. A legal open-table shot potting both groups enters shooter-owned choice after settling, retains both pots and the turn, and supports keyboard, mouse, owned controller, pause and reconnect. Fouls/scratches never grant that choice. AI chooses automatically, places after scratches and advances intermediate series results automatically; the final result stays visible. Join/disconnect pages cache references and rebuild labels/navigation only when state changes.
