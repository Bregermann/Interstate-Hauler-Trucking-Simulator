# Truck Sumo

Two to four offline competitors, any mix of named human/AI sources. Last truck in the raised ring wins a heat; first to five wins the match. All final crossings in the same physics step are batched before deciding a winner: genuine simultaneous final ring-outs produce a no-point draw. Rematches preserve editable names, colors, sources and independent Easy/Normal/Hard AI.

Accelerate and steer with the established assigned movement controls; reverse brakes before backing up. The mapped action gives one3.8m/s forward shove with3.2s cooldown. The shove's own contribution cannot exceed13m/s forward speed, while physical collision momentum is preserved. It affects only your own grounded truck; PhysX supplies contact response with no second collision impulse, stationary counter or sustained-contact knockback multiplier. Missing the shove can carry you over the edge. Equal12kg baseline bodies, NWH wheel/suspension controls,8.5m/s normal motor limit,5m/s reverse,48degree steering and the same grip apply to every source.

The authoritative chassis center crossing the visible outer radius defines ring-out. A wheel or cosmetic part hanging over is insufficient; being airborne inside is legal. Eliminated trucks immediately cease influencing competitors. Starts rotate each heat. A20s warning precedes shrinking at23s: actual collider, visual surface/rim and legal radius shrink from12m toward5m at.16m/s. An unresolved90s heat is an explicit no-point draw and replay, followed by a rotated reset.

Cosmetic modeled headbands, rear knots/tails, woven mawashi wraps and participant trim have no colliders. Rounded brows blend through neutral, focused, shove, impact, worried, relieved, victory and elimination poses with priorities and minimum holds. Edge recovery persists until safely inward. Terminal poses snap before the final replay sample. Real child transforms/scales and the shrinking ring are recorded; render-only replay ghosts have no physics/scoring authority. Three camera angles, half/normal/double speed, rewind and skip are available; AI sessions advance automatically.

Shared stable three-quarter arena camera, compact score/cooldown HUD, actual NWH engine/skid samples, contact-conditioned tire strain, project-owned cheer and shared audio/vibration settings. Pause, disconnect, focus loss and exit stop feedback; replacement requires neutral/fresh input. One AudioListener. The practical skin/camera images are generated natively with the scene.
