# Living kingdom ambience handoff

2026-10-28. This work owns only `Assets/TruckDragonKingdom/Ambience`, plus the separately authorized narrow Heist return-home landing correction. No Unity was launched, no CLEAN file or Git ref was written, no accepted world/road/ground was changed, and no ledger acceptance is inferred.

Call `LWS.TruckDragonKingdom.Ambience.DragonKingdomAmbienceAuthoring.Install(DragonKingdomWorld world)` in the explicit stopped new-mode composition, before saving it. The method returns `DragonKingdomAmbience`. The world must already pass its original reference and physical acceptance gates. Existing ambience is validated and rebound without replacing its populations. The installer creates no level, road, terrain, dragon or new listener.

The installer serializes the actual sole `SportsEventSession` found in the same scene and its four truck transforms as observers. Runtime `Bind(SportsEventSession owner, Transform[] focus = null)` is also public for an explicit lifecycle integration. The component follows the bound session's `Live`/`Paused` state, uses the existing `SportsMatchAudioAnchor`/single listener and SFX setting, and registers itself once in `world.resettableComponents` as `IDragonWorldResettable`. The existing `ResetWorld(seed)` therefore resets the same population at the ordinary new-match boundary. `SetPaused(bool)` and `StopAmbience()` are exposed for root-owned additional hooks; no second match clock is created. A static preview composition with no session deliberately does not run ambient simulation.

The finite population is five ordinary villagers (two village, one castle, harbor and refuge), a deer and rabbit in the forest, a real wolf in the mountains, and seven genuine animated birds across the outdoor regions. Caverns and the volcanic crater receive no biologically implausible bird circuit. All nine regions receive a real spatial audio source and retain their previous ambient anchors; the new usable ground/air waypoint transforms are appended. No gameplay scoring, combat targeting, ragdoll collisions or historical Plowing category presentation is added.

Original Taxi Girl 11/22 appearances are instantiated through the established `SportsAudienceAuthoring.Spawn` adapter, preserving their original Generic avatars and exact UTS `Girl no Heel controller.controller`, with its actual `idle1`/`walk` clips. Native V78 exposed that the prior Human-avatar assumption/Yard controller selection was inappropriate; a source-only correction now preflights both actual Generic profiles, exact clip bindings and bilateral leg articulation before composition. Purchased source meshes, materials, avatars and animation files remain unchanged. No Plowing category variants, badges, costume changes or categorical colors are used. Original albedo textures/colors, normal maps and cutout settings are retained in new local URP configuration where needed; already-correct URP materials are reused. Initial hidden renderers are excluded from the culling set so visibility does not expose disabled source parts.

Wildlife reuses the saved articulated `Original animated Deer/Rabbit.prefab` and `CampLivingArt` bone presentation, plus `TruckFishing/CarnivalPresentation/Genuine animated ambient wolf.prefab`. `CampWildlife` itself is not attached because it requires `CampingWilderness.session.explorers`, a different game authority. The small new adapter owns habitat semantics only; Unity `NavMeshAgent` retains path following/avoidance and the original actual rig/Animator or `CampLivingArt` retains deformation. Each actor gets its own small physically baked Navigation patch, never a kingdom-wide replacement surface. The installed `com.unity.ai.navigation` remains 2.0.12, unchanged.

Existing Quaternius Eagle/bird FBXs and their compatible saved Generic flight controllers supply actual articulated wing motion. The installed DOTween closed-path presentation mechanism, already used by the Sports audience, owns their continuous movement. Closed loop endpoints are identical. Runtime culling pauses paths at their current position; it does not warp, respawn or restart birds during play. Only an ordinary new-match reset restores authored seed poses.

Explicit route acceptance samples actual ground every 0.7 m, requires upright support and clear body volume, rejects road corridors including shoulders plus 12 m clearance, and stays away from truck starts, hoards, nests, perches and extraction approaches/zones. The ground ring must remain supported over every segment. Baked NavMesh paths must be complete and stay clear of road/objective boundaries. Separate habitat footprints avoid overlapping patches. Air loops use actual regional ambient hints, swept sphere checks and clearance from rider checkpoints/starts. Failure stops installation with a concrete region/habitat error; it does not fabricate a path, flatten ground or place a floating actor. Newly introduced dynamic obstructions cause native ground movement or bird tween progression to yield in place. Ambient actors have no colliders or rigidbodies and cannot obstruct actual objectives or NWH trucks.

The maximum population is 15. Distance culling stops Navigation/DOTween and rig animation outside the 280 m observer/camera radius, while retaining the same objects. Eight small NavMesh datasets are retained and unloaded by this component; no runtime bake occurs. Audio targets the three nearest eligible regions, with short bounded crossfades; there are only nine sources total. Existing Weather Maker `WeatherMakerLoopingAudioSource` owns fades. Native 3D logarithmic attenuation is used, Doppler is disabled, and all sources respect shared SFX volume. Castle crowd uses a sparse existing actual crowd cue at low volume, separated by at least 45 seconds rather than a continuously repeating cheer. Cavern native Cave reverb supplies actual acoustic response. Wind/thunder/weather, dragon action sounds, gold impacts, destruction, sheep and victory/battle music retain their mode/weather/audio owners; this addition does not claim their separate acceptance.
