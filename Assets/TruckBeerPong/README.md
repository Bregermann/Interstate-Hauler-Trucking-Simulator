# Truck Beer Pong

The truck is the projectile. Drive onto the outlined flush spring pad, hold the assigned ACTION to charge, steer to aim, then release to launch. The visible spring compresses and releases once. Identical bounded power and finite horizontal aftertouch apply to humans and AI. Release fore/aft once to arm range correction. First table/cup contact ends aftertouch; driving cannot resume after launch. Real tabletop bounces and hollow plastic rims remain physical.

1v1 or2v2 sequential hot seat; shared sources or individually assigned keyboard clusters/controllers/shared sticks. Only the current shooter controls the truck. Each team starts with ten cups. Teams alternate after either hit or miss; teammates alternate their own team's shots. Downward entry through an opposing live cup's open top plus0.65m interior penetration removes one cup. Direct, rim-in and table-bounce shots each remove one. Side/bottom/exterior/overflight and duplicate scores are rejected. Gaps remain. Cups stay anchored upright.

Clearing the last cup gives the opponent rebuttal: keep shooting while hitting, first miss loses, clearing the other rack starts three-cup overtime. No counter-rebuttal. Overtime alternates the opening side and repeats the same rebuttal rule. Solo practice is unlimited, with quick retry and rerack only after clearing all ten. No drinking mechanics or networking.

Pause preserves charge/flight/cups; charge resumes only after neutral and a fresh hold, so modal release cannot accidentally launch. Disconnect/replacement uses the collection's ownership gate. Rematch retains setup. Actual-motion render-only replay includes the truck, spring and cup visibility, with three angles,0.5/1/2x, rewind and skip. Scored truck nose-down splash is cosmetic/noncolliding; the cup and truck are cleared before the next shot.35s approach/charge and16s flight bounds prevent stalls; quiet landed bodies require a stable1.4s observation.
