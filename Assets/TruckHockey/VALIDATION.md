# Truck Hockey validation

Current 3v3 validation and delivery are in ../TruckSports/UPDATE_3V3.md. The former 161-check 1v1 suite is historical and was replaced with six-participant ownership, routing, physical-goal, recovery and sustained-play checks. Whole-controller exclusivity and separate left/right-stick ownership replace the old blanket prohibition on sharing a controller.

Physical scoring tests require completed PhysX puck crossings, not injected score events. Six-AI sustained runs use full two-minute regulation plus ordinary countdowns/goal pauses and repeated rematches. Software devices demonstrate source routing; actual physical controllers, keyboard rollover, audio listening and physical rumble require hardware playtesting.

Rules and contact authority are unchanged: two scores represent TEAMS, while all participant/spawn/readiness/contact/AI/recovery queries now cover six slots. Controls: README.md. Vendor audit/use/custom boundaries: ../TruckSports/README.md.
