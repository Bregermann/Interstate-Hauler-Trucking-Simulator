# Truck Snake: starter convoy, frequent pickups and length scoring

October 19 update, requested during the ongoing collection repair pass.

- Each active participant starts with **one actual pooled trailer**, visible during the countdown. Starting cargo is not a collected pickup and gives no free pickup points.
- The short straight starting bay is checked for hull clearance and road support before attaching it. Beyond that bay, trailers use the real driven 3D history, including delayed jump waves. Rematches clear the old train and restore exactly one starter trailer; unused participants have no active train.
- The saved scene and authoring defaults use **64 gates** (56 green, 8 gold), up from 24. Road candidates are sampled every **22 metres**, including actual roads inside the original city. The non-road stunt gap and roadside stop stubs are excluded.
- Gates can be spaced 22 metres apart instead of 40. They remain at least 18 metres from active tractors when spawning, so replenishment does not appear beneath a player. The refill interval is **0.5 seconds**, previously 2 seconds. Standard contact still grants one trailer; gold grants three. Clearance tests remain active.
- **Score multiplier = actual attached trailer count, with a minimum of x1.** It applies to pickup/combo rewards, distance, survival and landed jumps. One attached trailer earns x1, ten earn x10. Cargo waiting for sufficient clear path is excluded until visibly attached. The HUD and standings show actual length and the multiplier.
- Base rewards remain 100 per collected trailer plus the current combo reward, one point per 10 metres, one point per surviving second, and bounded jump rewards. These are then multiplied by actual attached length. Starter cargo does not alter collected-trailer statistics.
