## All-sport acceptance contract

Up to eight AI where appropriate. No random opponent scores, decorative-only trailers or generic racing substitutions. No silently dropped humans; teams/rounds/hot-seat are acceptable when appropriate.

Preserve SportsEventSession, SportsParticipantRegistry, SportsControllerKeyboard, typed results, SportsSessionTelemetry.GameplayEvent, Heat/uGUI, TMP, DOTween, Input System, profile persistence, official winner points and session statistics/report schemas. Stable IDs, ready-up, host sport selection, profile/controller ownership, result attribution, statistics, bounded telemetry, rematch and return-to-lobby must work without rejoining.
