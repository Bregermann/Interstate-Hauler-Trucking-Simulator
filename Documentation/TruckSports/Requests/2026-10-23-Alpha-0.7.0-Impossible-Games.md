# GAME 1 — TRUCK TRUCK GOOSE

## Concept

A ridiculous semi-truck version of Duck Duck Goose.

Eight to twelve trucks sit in an enormous circle.

One truck drives around selecting a competitor to become the GOOSE.

Both trucks race in opposite directions to claim the vacant parking spot.

## Environment

Build a giant whimsical playground featuring:

- Circular truck driving path.
- Truck-sized parking spaces.
- Massive slides.
- Giant swings.
- Sandpit.
- Grass.
- Colorful playground scenery.
- Animated spectators.
- Bright, attractive lighting.

## Gameplay

1. One truck becomes the picker.
2. Other trucks wait around the circle.
3. Picker drives around the perimeter.
4. Picker selects GOOSE with a dedicated interaction.
5. Goose becomes active.
6. Both race in opposite directions.
7. First legal arrival claims the vacant spot.
8. Loser becomes the next picker.

Allow appropriate physical competition and legal shortcuts.

Prevent skipping required race progression through exploits.

## Scoring

- Successful spot claim: +1.
- Highest score wins after the selected number of rounds.
- Optional elimination variant.

## AI

AI must select, react, race, claim spaces, and recover from collisions.

## Presentation

Include giant GOOSE announcements, cute truck reactions, crowd cheering, and lively playground audio.

## Acceptance

Verify selection, opposite-direction racing, valid parking claims, score awards, AI, and match completion.
