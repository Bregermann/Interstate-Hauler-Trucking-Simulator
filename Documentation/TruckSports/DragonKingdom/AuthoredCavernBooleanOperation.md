# Candidate authored cavern Boolean operation

Implementation is experimental and opt-in. No canonical world, vendor source, production default or installed build is changed. The coordinator owns the explicit main-loop wiring and native execution. Source compilation or bounded job controls cannot establish cave appearance or full-world acceptance.

## Evidence and proposed remedy

V378's five smooth-normal controls and saved-view test passed, with 421 original-layout mesh copies restored exactly. The actual entry image still shows regular ribs/grid and abrupt joins. This rejects a persistent smooth-normal pipeline as the repair. V379's two-pass additive excavation also passed the existing physical/source checks while retaining the unwanted appearance.

Read-only saved Cave04 analysis reconstructs 8,889 overhead grid-edge crossings directly from source-hashed native `.vox3` values. All corresponding 8,889 saved LOD0 grid vertices follow those interpolation positions, including Digger's existing `Chunk.cs` +0.001 m presentation offset. The residual float arithmetic difference is approximately 0.0000005 m. Median crossing fraction is 0.7423 toward the upper untouched endpoint; 1,056 exceed 0.9 and 183 exceed 0.99. These are actual geometric crossings, not merely mesh-normal shading. The complete script, byte hashes and individual measurements are retained under `Logs/TruckSports/DragonKingdom/CavernDensityBandsAuditV378`.

The analogous Cave14 density inventory has 8,743 overhead crossings and a lower median fraction (0.5559), consistent with the pressure change. Its simple minimum-vertex matcher also encounters layered/other-direction crossings and does not establish the same complete one-to-one match. The Cave04 result is the qualified complete matching evidence; neither inventory is a native runtime test or an acceptance-tolerance change.

Installed `BasicOperation.Dig` adds positive brush distance multiplied by original terrain depth and repeated pressure. Outside the brush it retains the original deep negative value. The candidate operation instead computes `max(currentValue, signedAuthoredField)` once. It applies the negative exterior band as well as the positive interior; writing only positive samples would retain the discontinuity under investigation. Repeated union is idempotent and no terrain-depth amplification is used. This is a substantive field-composition experiment; it is not yet a visually accepted remedy.

## Existing vendor foundation and scope

The installed public `IOperation<T>` and `DiggerSystem.ModifyWithoutMeshes` support project-owned job semantics. `EasyOverhangsOperation` is the actual installed example. The adapter follows that lifecycle: copy native chunk arrays, schedule an `IJobParallelFor`, return modified values through the public completion callback, use `Utils.AdjustAlteration` and `Cutter.Cut`, then let Digger perform mesh generation, collider baking, undo, holes and persistence.

`BooleanCavernOperation` consumes existing `BasicOperation` source inputs. Sphere operands use the same native vertical-ellipsoid field formula and authored center/radii. Custom corridor operands use the actual vendor `CustomBrush.InputVoxels`, origin, dimensions and native lattice correspondence. No corridor mesher or replacement terrain engine is introduced. Every source input is validated before mutation and custom-input bytes are hashed before/after.

The write band is three actual maximum lattice steps around the signed boundary. Outside it the complete voxel payload is untouched. The analytic bounds and existing public operation area remain the authority; the negative corridor band is limited by the actual computed vendor input. The ordinary twenty-pass/default path remains unchanged. This mode must start from fresh original terrain voxels and replace every lobe/corridor additive operand; applying max on top of already amplified positive values cannot undo those values.

The source-derived area review for the qualified 1.5625/0.5/1.5625 grid requires no area-method change. The band is 4.6875 m. Public X/Z cut margins are 6.5625 m; even after conservatively subtracting one entire lattice step for center quantization, the 65 m spherical area covers the 69.6875 m signed-support radius with 0.3125 m remaining before whole-chunk expansion. Its Y area is larger than both actual 30/25 m vertical caps plus their band. Unit-scaled custom input uses full input counts as world half extents, while valid vendor samples occupy approximately half those counts times the lattice scale. The source-hashed formula/bounds witness is `AuthoredBooleanUnionReady02/negative-band-area-audit.json`; it qualifies these current inputs, not arbitrary future resolutions. The existing public area helper is retained unchanged.

The coordinator's main branch retains native mesh/collision build, all 11,946 floor/roof/clearance columns, roof mass, source identities, critical poses, physical art-placement checks and saved views. It rejects incompatible cubic, pressure or smoothing/paint-trial flags. The original floor Reset remains unchanged for the default path; the Boolean branch's measured signed-floor correction is described below. A later native render must decide whether the field change resolves the reported appearance. Max-union can retain genuine intersection creases; a separate blended-union recipe is not silently included here.

## Actual first union result and signed-floor correction

V384 executed all seven union job controls successfully. Its actual full excavation failed after 78.603 s: every one of the 11,946 floor and roof rays hit, but the floor was at approximately 105.3517–105.4862 m, outside the unchanged 105±0.3 m bound. Roof heights were approximately 145.300–168.047 m with minimum clearance 39.815 m. Source, terrain heights and outside holes remained exact. This is a real physical rejection, not a completed cave or a missing-ray problem.

The old Reset restores the original deep negative terrain field below 105 m, while the new Boolean chamber above it has positive field values around 30. That mismatched pair biases linear marching interpolation upward. For example, values −80/+30 on the 105/105.5 m edge cross at about 105.364 m. Increasing the acceptance tolerance would conceal the error.

The candidate-only `ApplyAuthoredCavernFloor` operation instead intersects the void with the exact signed halfspace: `min(currentValue, worldY−105)`. It acts after all union operands, within the original measured X/Z floor footprint. Its lower limit is the original footprint minimum (84 m); its upper limit is the plane plus three maximum lattice steps (109.6875 m on the qualified grid). Both sides of the actual zero crossing therefore use the same signed metric, while the lower extent also eliminates the original lower void. The public Digger AABB helper schedules the work, the existing native job carries the explicit floor mode, and Digger still owns meshes, collisions, holes and persistence. Original Reset behavior is unchanged outside this opt-in branch.

Seven additional scheduled native-job controls cover the plane's exact zero/interpolated height, different positive chamber values, payload idempotence, byte-exact outside-X/Z samples, upper-band exclusion, original lower-bound exclusion and preservation of already-solid values. The full-scene height and all other physical/source guards remain unchanged. These new controls and the repaired full candidate are native-pending; no new saved view is meaningful until the physical gate passes.
